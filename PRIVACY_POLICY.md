# Privacy Policy - ARCED Collect

**Effective Date:** December 2024
**Last Updated:** December 2024

## 1. Introduction

ARCED Collect is a fork of ODK Collect, customized for ARCED Foundation (www.arced.foundation). This Privacy Policy explains how ARCED Foundation collects, uses, protects, and shares personal information through the ARCED Collect application.

ARCED Collect is designed for data collection in challenging environments, primarily for humanitarian, research, and development purposes. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data.

## 2. What Information We Collect

### 2.1 Form Data
- **Submitted Forms:** All data you collect and submit through ARCED Collect is stored locally on your device and may be sent to servers you configure.
- **Draft Forms:** Partially filled forms are stored locally on your device until you submit or delete them.
- **Form Metadata:** Information about when forms were filled, their status, and basic technical information.

### 2.2 Usage Data
- **Analytics:** We collect anonymous usage data to help improve the application. This includes:
  - Feature usage patterns
  - Performance metrics
  - Error reports (excluding personal data)
  - Device information (model, OS version)

### 2.3 Device Information
- **Storage Access:** ARCED Collect needs access to device storage to read form definitions and save collected data.
- **Location Services:** If enabled in forms, ARCED Collect may collect location data as part of form responses.
- **Camera and Microphone:** Required when forms include media capture capabilities.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

### 3.1 Primary Purposes
- **Form Data Processing:** To store, process, and transmit the data you collect through forms.
- **Application Improvement:** To analyze usage patterns and improve the application based on user needs.
- **Technical Support:** To troubleshoot issues and provide better support.

### 3.2 Analytics Usage
Anonymous usage data helps us understand:
- Which features are most and least used
- Performance issues and bottlenecks
- User needs and feature requests
- Usage patterns across different environments

This information helps us prioritize development efforts and improve ARCED Collect for all users.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 4.1 Form Data Sharing
- **Local Storage:** Form data is stored locally on your device.
- **Server Transmission:** When you submit forms, data is sent to the servers you configure through the application settings.
- **Third-Party Services:** Form data may be transmitted to third-party services (like Google Drive, OneDrive, etc.) if configured for form submission.

### 4.2 Analytics Sharing
- **Anonymous Data:** Usage analytics are shared with the ARCED Foundation development team in aggregated, anonymous form.
- **No Personal Information:** Our analytics system does not collect or transmit personal information, names, email addresses, or other identifiable data.

### 4.3 Third-Party Services
ARCED Collect may use third-party services for:
- **Maps:** Google Maps or other mapping services for location-based features
- **Crash Reporting:** To help identify and fix application crashes
- **Analytics:** To understand how the application is used

All third-party services are selected for their privacy commitments and compliance with data protection standards.

## 5. Data Security

### 5.1 Local Data Protection
- Form data stored locally on your device is protected by the device's security features.
- We recommend using device security features like encryption, secure locks, and regular backups.

### 5.2 Transmission Security
- Form data transmitted to servers is encrypted using industry-standard protocols (HTTPS/TLS).
- We encourage you to configure secure servers for form submission.

## 6. User Rights and Controls

### 6.1 Data Access
- You can always access your collected data through the application.
- You can export, delete, or modify your data at any time.

### 6.2 Privacy Controls
- **Analytics:** You can choose whether to share anonymous usage data through the application settings.
- **Permissions:** You control which permissions ARCED Collect has on your device and can revoke them at any time through Android settings.
- **Data Deletion:** You can delete forms, instances, and application data at any time.

## 7. Children's Privacy

ARCED Collect is not designed for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. International Data Transfers

ARCED Collect may process and store data internationally. We take appropriate measures to ensure your data is protected regardless of where it's processed.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will:
- Update the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy
- Provide notice of material changes through the application or other reasonable means
- Continue to respect the privacy choices you've made

## Compliance Summary

### GDPR
- **Lawful Basis:** Performance of a task carried out in the public interest or in the exercise of official authority vested in you.
- **Data Subject Rights:** Users have rights to access, rectify, delete, and restrict processing of their data.
- **Data Protection Officer:** Not applicable for this open-source project.

### CCPA
- **Categories of Data:** Form data, usage analytics, device information
- **Collection Purpose:** Form processing, application improvement, technical support
- **User Rights:** Access, deletion, opt-out of data sharing

### 12. Open Source Nature

As an open-source application, ARCED Collect's code is publicly available. This means:
- Anyone can review the code to verify privacy practices
- The application can be self-hosted for complete control over data
- Development is community-driven and transparent
